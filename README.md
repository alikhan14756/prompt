## 🌟 Overview

**BodyVista 3D** is a next-generation 3D interactive human anatomy and physiology web platform deployed at [aivanolabs.site](https://aivanolabs.site). It brings **2,900 anatomical structures, 12 human body systems, 140 dynamic physiological demonstrations, and 6 microscopic topics** directly into modern mobile and desktop browsers with 60 FPS WebGL rendering, intuitive touch gestures, and zero backend requirement.

---

## 🫁 Key Modules & Features

| Module | Core Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| **Human Anatomy** | 2,900 structures classified across 12 organ systems and 7 body regions (Head, Neck, Thorax, Abdomen, Pelvis, Upper Limb, Lower Limb). Features single-organ isolation, progressive hiding, translucent ghosting, and boundary clipping. |
| **Living Anatomy (Life Rhythm)** | 140 dynamic physiological demonstrations: cardiac beating with real-time synchronized Lead II ECG waveform, respiratory lung volume expansion and diaphragm mechanics, oxygenated vs. deoxygenated blood circulation streams, and joint kinesiology. |
| **Microscopic Exploration** | 6 deep microscopic topics: alveolar gas exchange, renal nephron filtration, sarcomere muscular contraction, neural synapses, intestinal villi absorption, and arterial wall histology. |
| **Oral & Dental Workspace (DentalVista)** | Complete adult 32-tooth permanent dentition mapped to the international FDI numbering system, tissue hierarchy (enamel, dentin, pulp, cementum, periodontal ligament), and mandibular movements. |
| **Anatomy Challenge Quiz** | Practical interactive tests covering structure identification, 3D anatomical searching, and microscopic histology with instant feedback and scoring. |
| **3D Surgical & View Tools** | Virtual scalpel dissection, 3-axis cross-section plane cutting (Transverse, Sagittal, Coronal), explode assembly sliders, 3D surface pen markup, ruler measurements, orthographic/perspective camera toggles, and screenshot exports. |

## 📱 Mobile & Desktop Control Compatibility

- **Touch (Mobile & Tablet):**
  - **Single finger drag:** Smooth 3D rotation with upright clamping.
  - **Two-finger pinch:** Dynamic zoom in & zoom out.
  - **Two-finger drag:** Smooth panning.
  - **On-screen navigation pad:** Dedicated `+` / `−` zoom buttons, view cube gizmo for front/back/left/right/top/bottom alignment, and fit centering.
- **Desktop (Mouse & Keyboard):**
  - **Left-click drag:** Rotate scene.
  - **Right-click drag / Middle mouse:** Pan scene.
  - **Mouse wheel:** Smooth exponential zooming.
  - **Shortcuts:** `Ctrl+Z` (Undo), `Ctrl+Y` (Redo), `Esc` (Exit tool).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Rendering Engine:** [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) (r187) + WebGL2
- **Spatial Indexing:** `three-mesh-bvh` for real-time raycasting and collision detection
- **UI & State:** React 19 + Zustand
- **Bundler:** Vite
- **Deployment:** GitHub Pages at [aivanolabs.site](https://aivanolabs.site) via custom CNAME
